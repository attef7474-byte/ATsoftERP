Notes
Quantities are the recorded stock-adjustment values in the source product unit. The stored adjustment type
(Adjustment In / Adjustment Out) carries the direction, and the recorded quantity is always positive; the direction is
never inferred from the quantity sign and the quantity is never negated or absolute-valued. The stock-adjustment detail
contract stores no before or after quantity and no live inventory balance, so none is printed or derived. Location names
and movement business references are not supplied by the stock-adjustment detail contract, so they are omitted rather
than replaced with identifiers. Unit cost and currency are printed only when recorded on the line (typically Adjustment
In); no line value, document total, cross-currency total or valuation is computed. Workflow submission, approval,
rejection, posting and cancellation timestamps are printed only when stored; workflow actor identities are internal ids
and are omitted. Quantities with different or missing units are never summed. This document is not a ledger
reconciliation.
