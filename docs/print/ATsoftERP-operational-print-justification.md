## 1. Authority and closed scope

- The shared print engine (`apps/web/src/lib/print.ts`, `apps/web/src/components/print/PrintDocument.tsx`, `use-print-document.ts`, `print-runtime.tsx`) is the only operational document renderer.
- The owner-accepted report evidence remains closed. Do not re-audit the 97 report routes, rebuild the engine, or migrate accepted report pages again.
- Print is read-only. No schema, migration, permission, numbering, workflow, inventory, cost, production-execution, or business-transaction change may be introduced to make a document printable.
- A route may print only when this file records `BUSINESS_PRINT_JUSTIFICATION = APPROVE` for that surface before implementation.
- Every surface receives exactly one classification: `DOCUMENT`, `REGISTER`, `REPORT`, `SPECIAL_PRINT_FLOW`, or `NOT_PRINTABLE`.

## 2. Business print test

A surface passes only when all five answers are yes:

1. **Business artifact** — the printed paper has an operational purpose outside the application (handed to a technician, attached to a machine, signed, archived, sent to a supplier, filed with a transaction).
2. **Stable identity** — the artifact is identified by a business number or a stable business context, not a database/internal identifier.
3. **Canonical source** — one authoritative endpoint supplies the identity, context, lines, and values; print does not invent or recompute business facts.
4. **Independent paper** — the artifact is useful without application controls, navigation, filters, or interactive-only state.
5. **Safe scope** — the existing read permission and the authenticated company/branch context are sufficient; no new permission and no tenant weakening are required.

A page that only looks better on paper than on screen does not pass. A page whose data is a projection already covered by an accepted report is `COVERED`, not a new print surface.

## 3. Decision vocabulary

| Decision | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `APPROVE` | Business print is justified and implementation may start after the gate conditions are met. |
| `DEFER` | Justified in principle, but a source, detail-route, identity, or proof gap must close first. No implementation now. |
| `COVERED` | A legitimate print exists in the accepted report or special-flow set; do not create a second operational print. |
| `REJECT` | No legitimate standalone paper artifact; screen-only. |
| `SPECIAL` | Existing label/QR flow with its own dimensions and print path; never migrate into A4 document mode. |

Priority: `P0` Maintenance closure, `P1` inventory/transaction documents, `P2` production and operational records, `P3` master-data registers and system lists.
